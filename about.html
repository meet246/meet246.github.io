About

Cybersecurity graduate (Summa Cum Laude, GPA 3.93) from USF's Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computing, graduating May 2026.

I have hands-on SOC experience during my internship with ReliaQuest labs, investigating real alerts in the GreyMatter platform including lateral movement, privilege escalation, and network-based threats and documenting findings following NIST-aligned incident response playbooks.

That experience shaped how I approach security: understand the attacker's behavior first, then build the detection or defense around it. Outside of coursework, I build and break my own lab environments to keep learning hands-on.

I've built an enterprise Active Directory environment from the ground up (AD DS, GPOs, hardening), investigated simulated PSEXEC lateral movement and HTTP beaconing/C2 activity, and I'm currently expanding that lab with a self-hosted Wazuh SIEM writing my own custom detection rules and validating them against simulated attacks.

Certified: CompTIA Security+, CompTIA A+, AWS Cloud Practitioner, Microsoft MTA: Windows Server Administration Fundamentals.

Looking to start my career as a SOC Analyst or IT Security Analyst, with a longer-term interest in cloud security and IAM as I continue building depth in those areas.
