About

I'm a cybersecurity graduate of the University of South Florida (B.S., summa cum laude, 3.93 GPA) based in Tampa, FL. I'm pursuing IT support and SOC analyst roles, with identity and access management (IAM) as where I want my career to go.

Identity is the thread through my work. In my Active Directory lab I automated user onboarding and group management with PowerShell, applied least privilege, and hardened the domain with Group Policy and audit policies. I then built a Wazuh SIEM on top of it, wrote detection rules, and attacked it from Kali to see what lateral movement looks like from the defender's side.

I also interned as a Cybersecurity Analyst with ReliaQuest, investigating alerts in the GreyMatter SIEM and documenting incidents against NIST-aligned playbooks. Over two years in customer-facing operations roles taught me to stay clear and steady with people who need help fast, which is most of what IT support is.

What I'm looking for

  • IT support / help desk roles: troubleshooting, account and access management, onboarding.
  • SOC analyst roles: alert triage, log analysis, detection engineering.
  • Longer term: identity and access management (IAM).
